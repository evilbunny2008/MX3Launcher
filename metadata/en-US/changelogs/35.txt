Initial F-Droid release.

* Flat, multi-column app grid Home replacement for Android TV / Google TV.
* Top bar: clock, Wi-Fi status, system settings, launcher settings.
* Theme (system/light/dark) and background gradient presets.
* Per-app show/hide and reordering.
* Menu button on a focused app icon opens that app's System App Info page.
* Backup and restore of launcher settings, with multiple timestamped
  backups to choose from.
* Detects waking from standby and restores the launcher to the
  foreground if something else took over in the meantime.
